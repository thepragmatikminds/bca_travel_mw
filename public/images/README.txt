BCA TRAVEL — PHOTOS
===================
The site uses free photos from Pexels (https://www.pexels.com/license/ — free for commercial use).
They load directly from Pexels, so nothing needs to be uploaded.

To use YOUR OWN photo instead:
  1. Put the file in this folder, e.g.  public/images/johannesburg.jpg
  2. In src/config/site.js change that item's  image:  to  "/images/johannesburg.jpg"
Recommended sizes: hero 1920x1080, destinations 720x900, services 1000x625 (JPEG, under 300 KB).

If any photo fails to load, the site automatically shows a built-in illustration in its place.
